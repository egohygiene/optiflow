Evidence pipeline

From a directory to a defensible decision.

Every conclusion is traceable to durable observations. Coverage, uncertainty, and interruption are first-class outcomes—not details hidden behind a success message.

01

Inventory without interpretation

Discover files, capture filesystem identity, and probe media metadata before deciding what any relationship means.

Media/ scene-a.mov scene-a-copy.mov proxy-a.mp4
02

Prove exact relationships

Candidate groups become exact duplicates only after compatible evidence and full-content hashing agree.

9f86d081…a08 = 9f86d081…a08
03

Plan for human review

Emit versioned JSON and human-readable reports that describe proposed actions while preserving the evidence that supports them.

run.jsonreport.jsonplan.json

Safety boundary

Observation is not permission to mutate.

OptiFlow v0.1.x is deliberately read-only. It records what it saw, names what it could not prove, and stops at a reviewable plan.
