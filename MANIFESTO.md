# OptiFlow Manifesto

## Opening Declaration

Media carries memory, labor, context, and possibility. A storage decision is
never only a number of bytes.

## What We Believe

We believe understanding must come before optimization.

We believe exactness is a claim that must be proved, uncertainty is information
that must be preserved, and a proposal is not permission.

We believe powerful automation can remain calm, inspectable, local-first, and
answerable to the people whose files it affects.

## What We Commit To

- Observe before inferring.
- Prove before claiming.
- Plan before acting.
- Re-prove before mutating.
- Validate before committing.
- Record enough evidence to explain and recover.
- Keep private media local unless a person explicitly chooses otherwise.
- Make every integration cross a versioned, owned boundary.

## What We Refuse to Normalize

We refuse silent deletion, invented certainty, hidden coverage gaps, shell
expansion around untrusted paths, irreversible defaults, misleading success,
and infrastructure complexity presented as product maturity.

We refuse to infer the personal value of media from metadata, popularity,
quality scores, or an automated model.

## Invitation

OptiFlow invites contributors to make storage intelligence more rigorous and
more humane at the same time. Add capability by strengthening evidence,
authority boundaries, validation, and recovery—not by bypassing them.

## Closing Statement

Know what is actually on disk. Preserve what is not yet known. Change only what
has been explicitly and safely authorized.
