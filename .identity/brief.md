# OptiFlow identity brief

## Essence

OptiFlow turns exact duplicate-media evidence into a reviewable local plan. It
should feel precise, composed, and operationally safe: a tool for understanding
what is true before deciding what to change.

## Visual direction

Use the Ego Hygiene family structure with a distinct cyan technical accent. The
mark suggests two directed paths converging on an inspectable decision, not an
automatic deletion. Favor crisp geometry, clear spacing, and high contrast.

## Avoid

- trash cans, destructive-cleanup symbolism, and success claims before review;
- neon cyberpunk decoration or dense dashboard noise;
- marks that imply mutation happens without an explicit operator decision.
